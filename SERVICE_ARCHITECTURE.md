## 2. Dedicated Service Page Specifications

### 1. Gate Fabrication (`/services/gate-fabrication/`)
- **Category:** Gates
- **H1:** Custom Gate Fabrication in Bangalore
- **Key Offerings:** Modern sliding gates, swing gates, telescopic gates, CNC laser-cut decorative gates, bi-folding gates.
- **Materials:** Mild Steel (MS), Stainless Steel (Grade 304/316), Cast Iron accents, Wrought Iron styling.
- **Specifications:** Heavy-duty bearing hinges, smooth nylon roller tracks, anti-sag structural bracing, automated motor integration.

### 2. Window Grills (`/services/window-grills/`)
- **Category:** Security Grills
- **H1:** Custom Window Grill & Safety Grill Fabrication
- **Key Offerings:** Modern minimalist horizontal bar grills, geometric square section grills, box grills with planter ledge, French window security grills.
- **Safety Standard:** Maximum 4-inch gap for child safety, burglar-resistant anchor fastenings.

### 3. MS Gates (`/services/ms-gates/`)
- **Category:** Gates
- **H1:** Mild Steel (MS) Gate Fabrication in Bangalore
- **Key Offerings:** Cost-effective, high-tensile mild steel entrance gates, industrial boundary gates, community security gates.
- **Corrosion Protection:** 2-coat zinc phosphate anti-corrosive primer, industrial-grade epoxy or polyurethane (PU) powder coat.

### 4. SS Railings (`/services/ss-railings/`)
- **Category:** Railings
- **H1:** Stainless Steel (SS) Railing Fabrication in Bangalore
- **Key Offerings:** Balcony railings, internal & external staircase handrails, terrace safety barriers, toughened glass & SS pillar combinations.
- **Grade Standard:** Genuine AISI 304 / 316 Grade stainless steel, hairline satin or mirror chrome finish, non-rusting in outdoor conditions.

### 5. MS Railings (`/services/ms-railings/`)
- **Category:** Railings
- **H1:** Mild Steel (MS) Railing Fabrication in Bangalore

### 6. Staircase Fabrication (`/services/staircase-fabrication/`)
