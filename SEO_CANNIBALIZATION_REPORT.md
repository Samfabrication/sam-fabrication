## 1. Objective & Methodology
Keyword cannibalization occurs when multiple URLs on the same domain compete for identical search intents, causing SERP confusion, dilution of backlink equity, and erratic ranking fluctuations.

Under the 2026 Master Strategy, every primary commercial keyword is allocated a single, authoritative Canonical Owner URL. Supporting URLs target strictly differentiated sub-intents or informational queries and link upward to the canonical owner.
