## 1. Principles of Bangalore Local SEO (Anti-Doorway Policy)
To strictly comply with Google's Spam Policies regarding doorway pages and maintain high search engine trust, our location pages adhere to the following rules:
1. **Real Service Footprint:** Only locations within the actual operational service radius in Bangalore are created.
2. **Distinct Hyper-Local Content:** No copy-pasting of generic boilerplate with merely the city name swapped. Each page addresses the architectural context, residential vs commercial character, prevalent project types, and localized service availability of that specific Bangalore neighborhood.
3. **Proof of Work & Project References:** Where applicable, local project milestones (e.g. Hebbal residential gate, Manyata commercial railings, Whitefield villa carport) are highlighted.
4. **NAP Consistency:** Business Name, Address, and Phone Number match GBP across every location page.
