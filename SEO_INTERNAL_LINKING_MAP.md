## 1. Internal Linking Graph Rules
1. **Upward Authority Flow:** All blog posts and location pages link upward to their corresponding parent Service Page using descriptive, semantic anchor text (never raw URLs or "click here").
2. **Contextual Lateral Linking:** Service pages cross-link to adjacent complementary services (e.g., Gate Fabrication links to Window Grills and Railings).
3. **Conversion funnels:** Every page terminates in a direct path to `/contact-us/` or instant quote initiation.
4. **Breadcrumbs:** Breadcrumbs (`BreadcrumbList` schema) render on all subpages to provide clear hierarchical crawling trails for search spiders.
