## 1. Asset Strategy & Optimization Rules
1. **Ultra High-Resolution Assets:** All site images are rendered in 1200×750 / 1000×800 unblurred, high-definition photorealistic resolution with zero compression artifacts.
2. **Dual Modern Formats:** Next-gen `.webp` delivered via `<picture>` tags with `.jpg` fallbacks.
3. **Zero Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS = 0.00):** Every single `<img>` specifies explicit `width` and `height` dimensions.
4. **Performance Directives:**
   - Hero and above-the-fold images: `loading="eager"`, `fetchpriority="high"`, `decoding="async"`.
   - Below-the-fold images: `loading="lazy"`, `decoding="async"`.
5. **Entity-Rich Descriptive Alt Text:** Detailed, natural descriptions of materials (AISI 304 SS, IS 2062 MS), fabrication processes (CNC laser cut, TIG/MIG welding), and architectural applications.
