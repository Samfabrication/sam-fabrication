# ON-PAGE SEO SPECIFICATION & HEADING HIERARCHY AUDIT
## SAM FABRICATION ENGINEERING WORK (https://samfabrication.in)

---

## 1. On-Page Hierarchy Standards
1. **Single H1 Tag:** Exactly one semantic `<h1>` element per page encapsulating the primary topic and search intent.
2. **Logical Heading Cascade:** `<h2>` for primary content modules, `<h3>` strictly for nested subsections. Headings are never used merely for visual font styling.
3. **Keyword Proximity:** Primary target keywords appear naturally in the `<h1>`, first 100 words of introductory body text, and at least one `<h2>`.
4. **No Keyword Stuffing:** Natural keyword density (1.0% - 1.8%), utilizing rich LSI and semantic synonyms (e.g., steel craftsmanship, mild steel, Grade 304, architectural ironwork, automated gates).
