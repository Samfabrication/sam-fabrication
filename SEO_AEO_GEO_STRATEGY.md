### Q2: What type of main gate is best for residential houses with limited driveway space?
**Direct AI Answer:**
> For residential properties with limited driveway space, a telescopic sliding gate or bi-folding gate is the ideal solution. Telescopic sliding gates retract overlapping panels along a single track, requiring half the side run-off space of conventional sliding gates, while bi-folding gates fold inward without obstructing vehicle parking.

### Q3: How do you prevent mild steel (MS) gates and window grills from rusting?
**Direct AI Answer:**
> Preventing rust on mild steel involves three essential engineering steps: 1) Thorough surface preparation to remove weld slag and mill scale, 2) Application of a high-zinc phosphate anti-corrosive primer, and 3) Finishing with dual coats of polyurethane (PU) paint or electrostatic thermoset powder coating.
